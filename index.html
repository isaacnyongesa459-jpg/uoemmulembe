01WHO WE ARE

A COMMUNITY WITH PURPOSE

Many roots.
One strong community.

The University of Embu Mulembe Union is a home for Luhya students from across Kenya. We connect people, counties and the 18 Luhya subtribes through a shared commitment to belonging, representation and mutual support.

Our union exists to make student life more connected: preserving cultural identity, building opportunities, standing up for members and creating space for every person to contribute.
